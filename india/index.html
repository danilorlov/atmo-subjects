FAQ

Questions Indian parents ask us

Will my child need IELTS for a UK university?

Often not. Many UK universities accept IGCSE English Language as proof of English — for example, Edinburgh accepts grade C/4 and Manchester accepts IGCSE English as a First Language. Requirements vary by university and course — we'll go through your child's shortlist on the call.

What about the UK Student visa?

Your child needs an offer from a UK university, which confirms their English level for the visa. British qualifications and IGCSE English make that step simpler. The visa decision itself is made by UK Visas and Immigration.

Can my child switch from CBSE or ICSE?

Yes. The most common entry points are Class 9 (to start IGCSE) and Class 11 (to start A-Levels). On a free call we look at your child's current subjects and recommend where to start.

Where does my child sit the exams?

IGCSE and A-Level exams are taken at a registered exam centre. There are Cambridge and Pearson Edexcel exam centres across India, and we'll guide you through registration.

What if lesson times clash with school or coaching?

Every lesson is recorded, so your child can catch up the same day. Many students mix live lessons with recordings around their week.

How does ATMO help with UK university applications?

A-Levels are the qualification UK universities specify in their offers. As a UCAS Registered Centre we support applications end to end — subject choice, predicted grades, personal statement and offers.

How do I pay from India?

Fees are set in British pounds. We'll walk you through payment options and payment plans on your free call.

Not sure where your child should start?

Book a free 15-minute call. We'll map your child's current class to the right IGCSE or A-Level start, and the route to the UK universities you have in mind.
