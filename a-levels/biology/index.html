Exams and assessment

How Biology is assessed

Theory Papers (MCQ, structured, free response)

Our A Level Biology course includes both written and practical assessments, giving students a strong foundation in scientific knowledge and real-world skills.

Students complete five exam papers:

Paper 1 – Multiple Choice

Quick-fire questions to test understanding of key biology topics.

Paper 2 – Structured Questions

Short written answers explaining biological processes, definitions, and data.

Paper 3 – Practical Skills

In the laboratory – students carry out experiments, use a microscope, and record results.

Paper 4 – Advanced Questions

More challenging written tasks that assess deeper understanding and critical thinking.

Paper 5 – Planning & Analysis

Students design experiments, interpret results, and suggest improvements – no lab required.

All papers are externally marked by Cambridge International. Final grades reflect both theory and practical ability – excellent preparation for university-level study.

Practical or Alternative to Practical

On A Level Biology (9700), students gain valuable hands-on experience through a practical lab exam (Paper 3).

This subject follows the practical route only, as practical skills are an important part of studying biology.

Students also strengthen their scientific thinking in Paper 5, which focuses on planning and data analysis.

Regular internal tests and mocks

Students take regular topic-based tests and full mock exams to track progress and build exam confidence.

This helps identify strengths and areas for improvement well ahead of the final Cambridge exams.

Support: past papers, exam prep sessions, feedback

We support every student with access to past papers, guided exam preparation sessions, and personalised teacher feedback — all to help them feel confident and ready for success.

Can UK-based students take this course as a distance learning alternative?

Yes — this A-Level Biology distance learning course is used by students both outside and within the UK who need a flexible alternative to attending a physical sixth form, while following the same Cambridge International (CIE) syllabus and external exams.

Can I take just the AS Level, or do I need to commit to the full A Level?

You can study Cambridge AS Level Biology as a standalone qualification, or continue into the full A Level after completing the AS Level modules.
